# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day ship the same day. Weekends and public holidays are not counted as business days.

## Order tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update. Customers can also track orders from My Orders in their account.

## Delayed or lost parcels
If a parcel has not moved for 3 business days or is more than 5 business days late, we open an investigation with the carrier. If the carrier cannot find the parcel within 5 business days, we send a free replacement or a full refund, the customer's choice. Customers do not need to do anything, support will confirm by email.

## Shipping costs
Orders over 50 USD ship free with standard shipping. Below that, standard shipping costs 4.99 USD. Express shipping costs 12.99 USD. We currently ship to the US, Canada and the UK.
