# Billing and Payments

## Double charges
A temporary duplicate authorization can appear when a payment is retried. It disappears on its own within 3 to 5 business days and is not a real charge. If two completed charges appear for the same order, we refund the duplicate automatically within 5 business days after support confirms it. Customers should send the last 4 digits of the card and the order ID, never the full card number.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery or bank transfers.

## Invoices and receipts
An invoice PDF is emailed after every order. Past invoices can be downloaded from My Orders, then Invoice. Company name and tax ID can be added to the invoice within 30 days of the order by contacting support.

## Subscription billing
The ShopNova Plus subscription renews monthly on the day it was started. It can be cancelled anytime from Account, then Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and access continues until the end of the paid period. Renewals already charged are not refunded unless requested within 7 days.
