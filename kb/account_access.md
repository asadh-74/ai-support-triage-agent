# Account and Login

## Password reset
On the login page choose Forgot password and enter the account email. The reset link arrives within 5 minutes and is valid for 1 hour. If nothing arrives, check the spam or junk folder and make sure the email address is the one used to register. Requesting several resets in a row invalidates the earlier links, so only use the newest email.

## Account locked
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. Waiting 30 minutes or resetting the password unlocks it. Support cannot unlock an account earlier and cannot see or change a customer's password.

## Change email or delete account
The email address can be changed under Account, then Profile. To delete an account, go to Account, then Privacy, then Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes order history after 30 days.
