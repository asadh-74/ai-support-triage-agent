# Refunds and Returns

## Return window
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Sale items marked "final sale" and gift cards cannot be returned. To start a return, open My Orders, choose the order and select Return item. A prepaid return label is emailed within 1 hour.

## Refund processing time
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after our warehouse receives the returned item. The money goes back to the original payment method. Bank or card providers can take an extra 3 to 5 business days to show the refund on the statement. A confirmation email is sent when the refund is issued.

## Damaged or wrong item
If an item arrives damaged, defective or is not what was ordered, the customer will receive a free replacement or a full refund, their choice. No return is needed for items under 50 USD. Ask the customer to send a photo of the item and the packaging so we can file a claim with the carrier.
